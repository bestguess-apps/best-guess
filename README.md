# Best Guess

Free crypto odds, honestly. See which way a few coins are leaning, why, and how often calls like that have been right before.

**Open it:** https://bestguess-apps.github.io/best-guess/

- Your watchlist of coins, picked from the top 25 (stablecoins and wrapped copies left out), with prices that update live.
- Coming next: a 7-day forecast for each coin, with odds and a confidence level, a likely price range on a small fan chart, and key price levels. Best Guess's own engine makes the forecast by finding past look-alike setups and seeing what happened next.
- Coming after that: "Worth a look" (the day's strongest, best-tested leans) and a Track record that compares the engine with a plain coin flip, even when that looks bad.
- No accounts, ads or tracking. Your watchlist and settings save only on your device.
- Add it to your Home Screen and it works like an app, even offline, with the last prices it saw.

## Privacy, in plain words

- Saved prices come from this website. GitHub fetches them from CoinGecko about every 6 hours.
- For live prices, your phone asks CoinGecko for the latest prices of the coins on your watchlist. CoinGecko sees that request like any website visit, and nothing else. A built-in privacy lock stops the page from contacting any other site.

## Not financial advice

Best Guess's odds are a best guess from past prices. Past prices can't promise what comes next, and crypto can drop fast. Never put in money you can't afford to lose.

Best Guess is a one-person volunteer project, built with help from AI (Claude, made by Anthropic). Price data from [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com). Not affiliated with CoinGecko.
