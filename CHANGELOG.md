# Best Guess: changelog

## 0.2.0 (Nov 10, 2026)
- Each coin on your watchlist now shows its 7-day lean: up, down or "coin flip", with odds and a confidence level. Tap a coin for its full forecast: a best guess for a week from now, a likely price range on a small chart, the prices it keeps turning at (floors and ceilings), and the reasons in plain words.
- Best Guess's own engine makes the forecasts. It finds the 50 moments in the past year, across all the coins it follows, that looked most like today, and checks where prices were 7 days later. Odds stay close to 50-50 unless those look-alikes clearly agree, so most days the honest answer is "coin flip."
- Forecast prices are rounded (like $96,500), so they don't look more exact than they are.
- Edit coins: pick 1 to 10 coins from the top coins. Your picks save on this device.
- A Share button sends the official link, with nothing about you attached.
- About explains how the odds work.
- Fixes: a message at the bottom of the screen no longer blocks taps on what's under it, and coin names no longer get squeezed on small phones with extra large text.
- Tested: 11 engine tests passed on made-up markets, and 5 of 5 planted bugs were caught (seeing the future, an off-by-one, swapped floors and ceilings, a missing flat-price check, a weighting slip). Honesty checks: on 30 made-up markets with no real pattern, leans were right 49.5% of the time (a coin flip, as they should be) and High confidence almost never showed; with a planted pattern, Medium calls were right 72% of the time; the likely range held the real price 7 days later about 8 times in 10, as promised. Changing every price after a replay day changed nothing, so replays can't see the future. 54 browser checks passed in headless Chrome with made-up prices: forecasts on every row and sheet (live and saved prices, live prices failing, offline through the service worker), the fan chart and its text version, best guess inside the range and agreeing with the lean, coins with short, flat or missing history, a hostile coin name (plain text, nothing ran), Edit coins (up to 10, at least 1, saved after reopening, focus and Tab), Share with and without a share sheet, 0.1.0 saved data, damaged, wrong and newer price files, newer saved data left alone, tabs and About, no sideways scrolling or squeezed names at 320px with extra large text, no privacy-lock blocks, and 10 forecasts in under a second on a CPU slowed 6 times. The accessibility checker found nothing on 5 screens (main, a forecast, a coin with no forecast, Edit coins, About) at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast computed for 15 new color pairs in both themes; all pass. 21 fetcher tests still pass. Then on the live site: Publish ran the engine tests and passed, and on a real iPhone the forecasts looked right on real prices, and About → Data check showed saved prices (25 coins), live prices and settings all working.

## 0.1.0 (Nov 9, 2026)
- First version, for testing the plumbing (stage 1). Not shared yet.
- Today shows your watchlist (the top 5 coins to start) with each coin's price and how it moved in the last 24 hours. Prices come from this website, refreshed about every 6 hours, plus a live check with CoinGecko each time you open it.
- Works offline with the last prices it saw, installs to the Home Screen, follows light and dark mode, and has a text size button.
- About has a Data check that says whether saved and live prices are working, and why not if they aren't.
- Worth a look and Track record say what's coming.
- A price fetcher on GitHub gets the top 25 coins and a year of daily prices from CoinGecko's free keyless service, staying under its limit, stopping after 15 minutes if CoinGecko is struggling, and keeping the last good prices if anything fails.
- Tested: 21 fetcher tests passed, and 5 of 5 planted bugs were caught. A simulated publish run with CoinGecko unreachable kept going and published the app without prices (or with the last good prices when there were some). 49 browser checks passed in headless Chrome with made-up prices: live and saved prices, live prices failing two ways, missing, damaged, wrong and newer price files, a hostile coin name (shown as plain text, nothing ran), offline through the service worker, tabs with arrow keys, the About sheet with focus and Esc, text size, no sideways scrolling at 320px with extra large text, and the privacy lock blocking other sites. The accessibility checker found nothing on the main screen, About, the other tabs and the "prices didn't load" screen, at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast computed for 15 color pairs in both themes; all pass. Then on the live site: the first publish got all 25 coins with a year of history from CoinGecko in 26 requests, and on a real iPhone, About → Data check showed saved prices (25 coins) and live prices both working.
