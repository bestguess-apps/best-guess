# Best Guess: changelog

## 1.2.0 (Nov 12, 2026)
- New: forecasts are easier to understand. Everything new is about how much to trust today's guess, never what to do with a coin.
- "In short" at the top of every forecast: one plain sentence rolling up the lean, how confident it is and how past leans did, like "Leaning up a little, but its past leans were right 52% of the time, about what a coin flip gets. Treat it as a hint." It says so plainly when past leans did no better than a coin flip, or no better than just guessing up (or down) every time.
- A made-up $100: what $100 in that coin would likely be worth in a week, downside first, and the price it'd fall below about 1 time in 10. Same numbers as the likely range.
- Last week's call: what Best Guess said for that coin 7 days before its latest daily price, and whether it turned out right and inside the likely range. It's worked out the same way the Track record works out every call, with no peeking at what came after.
- Big swings: a note when a coin has been moving much more than usual from day to day, which also makes its likely range wider.
- Before you decide: a fold-out near the bottom of every forecast with 3 questions about you, not the coin: could you lose all of it, will you need the money soon, and would a 30% drop next week be okay.
- "Not financial advice" on every forecast is now shorter and comes with two real facts from that coin's past year of daily prices: its biggest 7-day drop, and how often it was lower a week later.
- About explains the new parts.
- Nothing new leaves your device, and the privacy lock still allows only this site and CoinGecko.
- Tested: engine and publish-step tests: 41 passed (8 new: In short's words for every case, its honesty test, last week's call matching the Track record's latest call, last week's call never peeking, no call to check, the big swing bar, and the past-year facts counted by hand), and the honesty tests still pass. On made-up markets with no real pattern, In short said "better than a coin flip" on about 1 lean in 20 (the test fails above 1 in 10); the big swing note showed on about 1 day in 20. Fetcher tests: 21 passed. 7 of 7 bugs planted in the engine were caught (the easy guess not checked, "worse" never said, the coin-flip band stretched, too few of a coin's own leans used, last week's call peeking 7 days ahead, the big swing bar lowered, more than a year of prices used), and 4 of 4 planted in the app were caught by the self-test (wrong confidence words in In short, the $100 line from the best guess instead of the 10% line, the big swing note never showing, the facts' wording). ## 1.1.0 (Nov 12, 2026)
- New: Best Guess tells you when its saved prices are old. They normally update every 6 hours. If they're 2 days old, the top of the app turns yellow and says so, even when live prices are working, because forecasts are built on the saved price history. At a week old, forecasts and Worth a look pause until prices update, since a forecast would treat a week's move as one day's. The Track record still shows, and About → Data check says the prices are out of date.
- New: the easy guess in the Track record. Next to "Leans that were right" it now shows how often just saying "up" (or "down", whichever happened more) every time would have been right on those same days. When prices mostly move one way, beating a coin flip is easy, so this is a second bar to compare with. It's only shown: it doesn't change any verdict, confidence level or Worth a look pick.
- Behind the scenes: GitHub pauses scheduled updates in public repos after 60 days without changes. The Publish workflow now turns its own schedule back on once a day, so prices keep updating even when nothing else changes. It runs none of the app's code and has no other new permissions. If it ever stops working, the new warning says so.
- Nothing new leaves your device, and the privacy lock still allows only this site and CoinGecko.
- Tested: engine and publish-step tests: 33 passed (3 new: the easy guess, old prices, and planted bugs), and the honesty tests still pass. Offline through the service worker, the app opened with the last prices. The workflow checker (actionlint 1.7.12) found nothing in either workflow. Security release check: no personal details, debug lines or new requests; the keepalive job has `actions: write` in its own job only, runs no repo code and no outside actions, and passes values through env. ## 0.4.0 (Nov 10, 2026)
- New: the Worth a look tab. Each day Best Guess goes through all 25 coins it follows, not just your watchlist, and names up to 3 whose 7-day leans stand out, strongest first. When none do, it says "Nothing stands out today" and tells you why. Most days, few or none will make it.
- The bar is strict on purpose: a lean needs medium or high confidence, and past leans like it must have been right at least 55% of the time (that coin's own leans when there are enough to tell, otherwise all coins together). With no track record to test against, nothing is named.
- Each pick shows its lean, the odds, its confidence, and how often its past leans were right. When two or three show up, it reminds you that coins tend to move together, so they're closer to one call than three.
- Tap a pick for its full forecast. If the coin isn't on your watchlist, an "Add to my coins" button puts it there (up to 10 coins).
- About explains how Worth a look works.
- Fix: when a forecast closes and its row is no longer on screen, the keyboard and VoiceOver focus now goes to the open tab instead of getting lost.
- Nothing new leaves your device. Worth a look uses the saved prices, and the live price check still asks only about your watchlist.
- Tested: 25 engine tests passed on made-up markets (5 new), and 5 of 5 planted bugs in Worth a look were caught (a missing confidence bar, a backwards order, a down lean ranked by its up odds, a missing limit of 3, a so-so record let through). ## 0.3.0 (Nov 10, 2026)
- New: the Track record tab. For each of the last 90 days, Best Guess replays the call it would have made using only the prices up to that day, then checks the real price 7 days later. It shows the results as they are, good or bad.
- For all coins together and for each coin, it shows: how often leans were right next to a plain coin flip, how often it said "coin flip" instead of leaning, results by confidence level, how often the price landed inside the likely range (the promise is about 8 in 10), and how far off the best guess was next to just assuming "no change."
- It says "too few to tell" when there aren't enough calls (at least 10 leans from 4 separate weeks), and it says when a result could still be luck. Calls a day apart overlap and coins move together, so 90 days is only about 13 separate weeks.
- The track record now holds confidence down. A coin whose leans were right less than half the time stays at low confidence, and none can say high unless its leans were right at least 55% of the time. A forecast says so when this happens. It never raises confidence or changes a lean.
- Each forecast has a new "Its track record" line, and About → Data check says whether the track record loaded.
- The replay (about 2,250 forecasts) is worked out on GitHub each time prices update and comes along with the saved prices, so it's instant on any phone and works offline. Nothing new leaves your device.
- Tested: 20 engine tests passed on made-up markets (9 new), and 4 of 4 planted bugs were caught (a replay whose look-alikes peek 30 days ahead, prices that run 3 days past the call, a backwards score, a missing confidence cap). Changing every price after a replay day changed no earlier call. Scores matched a hand count and a second, separate replay, and each coin's counts added up to the totals. On made-up markets with no pattern the record never claimed to beat a coin flip; with a planted pattern leans were right more than 56% of the time. 5 new tests for the publish step passed (junk coins skipped, safe to run twice, a failure leaves the file untouched), and 21 fetcher tests still pass. 117 browser checks passed in headless Chrome with made-up prices: the Track record at 320px, 390px and desktop, light and dark, with extra large text; every kind of result on a hand-made record (better, worse, could be luck, too few, no leans, no record); confidence held down on real forecasts with the reason shown; a missing record, six kinds of damaged record (including one carrying code, which was ignored) and no prices at all; keyboard use, open coins staying open through a refresh and a watchlist change; and offline through the service worker. No sideways scrolling, no page errors, no requests to other sites, privacy lock on throughout. The accessibility checker found nothing on 7 screens (main, Track record, Track record with coins open, Track record not ready, a forecast, Edit coins, About) at 4 sizes in light and dark. Security release check: nothing found. Not yet checked on a real iPhone or with VoiceOver, or against real prices. Later on Nov 10, the owner checked the live site at 0.4.0 on a real iPhone (About and Data check, Worth a look, Track record, VoiceOver) and reported everything looked good.

## 0.2.0 (Nov 10, 2026)
- Each coin on your watchlist now shows its 7-day lean: up, down or "coin flip", with odds and a confidence level. Tap a coin for its full forecast: a best guess for a week from now, a likely price range on a small chart, the prices it keeps turning at (floors and ceilings), and the reasons in plain words.
- Best Guess's own engine makes the forecasts. It finds the 50 moments in the past year, across all the coins it follows, that looked most like today, and checks where prices were 7 days later. Odds stay close to 50-50 unless those look-alikes clearly agree, so most days the honest answer is "coin flip."
- Forecast prices are rounded (like $96,500), so they don't look more exact than they are.
- Edit coins: pick 1 to 10 coins from the top coins. Your picks save on this device.
- A Share button sends the official link, with nothing about you attached.
- About explains how the odds work.
- Fixes: a message at the bottom of the screen no longer blocks taps on what's under it, and coin names no longer get squeezed on small phones with extra large text.
- Tested: 11 engine tests passed on made-up markets, and 5 of 5 planted bugs were caught (seeing the future, an off-by-one, swapped floors and ceilings, a missing flat-price check, a weighting slip). Honesty checks: on 30 made-up markets with no real pattern, leans were right 49.5% of the time (a coin flip, as they should be) and High confidence almost never showed; with a planted pattern, Medium calls were right 72% of the time; the likely range held the real price 7 days later about 8 times in 10, as promised. Changing every price after a replay day changed nothing, so replays can't see the future. 54 browser checks passed in headless Chrome with made-up prices: forecasts on every row and sheet (live and saved prices, live prices failing, offline through the service worker), the fan chart and its text version, best guess inside the range and agreeing with the lean, coins with short, flat or missing history, a hostile coin name (plain text, nothing ran), Edit coins (up to 10, at least 1, saved after reopening, focus and Tab), Share with and without a share sheet, 0.1.0 saved data, damaged, wrong and newer price files, newer saved data left alone, tabs and About, no sideways scrolling or squeezed names at 320px with extra large text, no privacy-lock blocks, and 10 forecasts in under a second on a CPU slowed 6 times. The accessibility checker found nothing on 5 screens (main, a forecast, a coin with no forecast, Edit coins, About) at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast computed for 15 new color pairs in both themes; all pass. 21 fetcher tests still pass. Then on the live site: Publish ran the engine tests and passed, and on a real iPhone the forecasts looked right on real prices, and About → Data check showed saved prices (25 coins), live prices and settings all working.

## 0.1.0 (Nov 9, 2026)

- Today shows your watchlist (the top 5 coins to start) with each coin's price and how it moved in the last 24 hours. Prices come from this website, refreshed about every 6 hours, plus a live check with CoinGecko each time you open it.
