About Best Guess

Version

Best Guess shows which way a few coins are leaning, why, and how often calls like that have been right before. It's free, with no ads, accounts or tracking.

Not financial advice

Its odds are a best guess from past prices. Past prices can't promise what comes next, and crypto can drop fast. Never put in money you can't afford to lose.

How the odds work

For each coin, Best Guess sizes up today's setup: the price against its 20- and 50-day averages, how far it moved in the last 7 and 30 days, how much it's swinging, and where it sits in its 30-day range. Then it finds the 50 moments in the past year, across all the coins it follows, that looked most like that, and checks where each price was 7 days later.

Odds stay close to 50-50 unless those look-alikes clearly agree. Under 55% either way, it says "coin flip", and most days that's the honest answer. Confidence (low, medium or high) depends on how strongly they agree and how many different weeks they came from. Coins tend to move together, so ten coins on the same day count as one example.

Worth a look goes through every coin Best Guess follows and names up to 3 whose leans stand out: medium or high confidence, and past leans that were right at least 55% of the time (that coin's own when there are enough to tell, otherwise all coins together). When none do, it says "Nothing stands out today." Coins tend to move together, so two or three names on the same day are closer to one call than three.

The Track record tab shows how its calls really did over the last 90 days, good or bad. That record also holds confidence down: a coin whose leans were right less than half the time stays at low confidence, and none can say high unless its leans were right at least 55% of the time.

Your privacy

  • Your watchlist and settings save on this device only. Nothing about you is sent anywhere.
  • Saved prices and the track record come from this website, refreshed about every 6 hours.
  • For live prices, your phone asks CoinGecko for the latest prices of the coins on your watchlist. CoinGecko sees that request like any website visit, and nothing else.
